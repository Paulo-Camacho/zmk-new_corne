Apply to keyboard

Combos are compiled into firmware, so applying them means commit → push → CI build → flash.
  1. Commit & push the keymap
    Only config/eyelash_corne.keymap is staged.
  2. CI build
    Waiting for a pushed commit.
  3. Clear saved settings (optional)
    Only needed to wipe Studio layer overrides and Bluetooth bonds. To change or remove combos but keep keybinds, use Skip and flash normal firmware below.
  4. Clear saved settings — right half
    Plug in the right half, double-tap reset, then flash settings reset.
  5. Flash the left half
    Double-tap reset on the left half (studio firmware).
  6. Flash the right half
    Combos on N/M/, etc. live on the right — flash this half too or old combos may still work.

Binding reference

A binding is &behavior plus its parameters — &kp ESCAPE presses Escape, &mo 1 holds layer 1.
